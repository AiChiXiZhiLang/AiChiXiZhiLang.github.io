Privacy policy

Last updated: 2026-11-24

optimizer-audit-backup is a personal-use command-line tool operated by a single owner. It uses Google OAuth solely to copy files between the owner's own computers and the owner's own Google Drive folder.

Data accessed

The app requests only the OAuth scope drive.file, which grants access solely to files and folders the app itself creates in the owner's Google Drive. It cannot read other Drive content, email, profile, or any other Google service data.

Storage and sharing

Files are transferred directly between the owner's machines and the owner's Google Drive. No data is sent to, stored on, or shared with any third party or external server. OAuth tokens are stored locally on the owner's machines and are used only for this purpose.

Deletion

The owner may delete archived files at any time in Google Drive and may revoke the app's access at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions. To request deletion of any remaining data, contact the address below.
